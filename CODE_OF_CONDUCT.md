# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Being respectful and inclusive
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback
- Assuming positive intent and staying curious

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, hate speech, or trolling
- Personal attacks
- Publishing private information without consent

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer via GitHub issues or profile contact details.

Instances of abusive behavior may result in warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban from project spaces.
